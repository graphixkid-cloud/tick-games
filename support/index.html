Common questions

How do I put Tick Games on my child’s watch?

Download it from the App Store on the iPhone paired with the watch. If the watch is set up with Apple Watch for Your Kids, you can also get it from the App Store on the watch itself.

Does it need Wi‑Fi or a cellular plan?

No. Every game plays offline, and the app makes no network connections.

Where is my child’s progress saved?

Stars and levels are saved on the watch. Deleting the app deletes that progress.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. You buy Tick Games once, and that’s it. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Who can follow links in the app?

Only grown-ups. Links to the web sit behind a parental gate in the iPhone app.
